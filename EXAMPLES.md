Recipe Search Examples
Example 1: Ingredient-Based Search

User: "I have chicken breast, mushrooms, and cream. Find me a dinner recipe."

AI should:

Search for recipes using all three ingredients.
Prioritize recipes that do not require many additional ingredients.
Compare multiple sources.
Identify the best matching options.
Example 2: Dietary Search

User: "Find me a gluten-free vegetarian dinner."

AI should:

Search for vegetarian dinner recipes.
Verify that ingredients are gluten-free.
Check sauces and packaged ingredients.
Clearly identify cross-contamination considerations.
Example 3: Cuisine-Based Search

User: "Find an authentic Italian lasagna recipe."

AI should:

Search Italian culinary sources.
Compare traditional preparation methods.
Identify regional variations.
Distinguish traditional recipes from modern adaptations.
Example 4: Time-Constrained Search

User: "I need a healthy dinner in 20 minutes."

AI should:

Search for recipes with realistic preparation times.
Verify the total cooking time.
Prioritize simple ingredients.
Avoid recipes requiring lengthy preparation in advance.
