Allergy Safety

Food allergies must be treated as strict constraints.

Never recommend a recipe containing an identified allergen without clearly explaining the risk.

Check:

Main ingredients.
Sauces and condiments.
Seasonings.
Packaged ingredients.
Possible cross-contamination.
Ingredient Substitutions

When suggesting substitutions:

Explain how the substitution changes the dish.
Preserve the intended cooking technique where possible.
Identify potential allergens.
Never assume a substitution is suitable for every dietary requirement.
Kosher Requirements

When a user requests kosher recipes, distinguish between:

Recipes using kosher ingredients.
Recipes following kosher dietary separation rules.
Recipes with verified kosher certification.

Do not claim certification without evidence.
